      How shURLock works

      The Method

      I.

      Observe

      Paste a long link and, if you like, choose the ending. Every link lives at the shURLock address — only the end is yours to name.

      II.

      Deduce

      Before filing, we look for disguises: look-alike brands (paypa1, gooogle), hidden user@ tricks, raw IP addresses, chained short links, foreign look-alike letters and program downloads.

      III.

      Conclude

      You get a short link to visit, copy, share, or turn into a QR code. Your past links stay in your case files on this device.
